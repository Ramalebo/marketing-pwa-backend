## 📧 Step 3: Register for Mailtrap (Email Marketing)

**Time: 5 minutes**

1. Go to: **https://mailtrap.io**
2. Click "Sign Up" and create a free account
3. Verify your email address (check your inbox)
4. Once logged in:
   - Go to **Sending Domains** in the left menu
   - Click **"Add Domain"** or use their test domain
   - If adding your own domain, follow the verification steps
   - If using test domain, skip to step 5
5. Click on your domain, then go to **Integrations** tab
6. Under **Transactional Stream**, click **"Integrate"**
7. Toggle to **SMTP** (not API)
8. Copy these credentials:
   - **Host**: `sandbox.smtp.mailtrap.io`
   - **Port**: `2525`
   - **Username**: (shown in dashboard)
   - **Password**: (shown in dashboard)
