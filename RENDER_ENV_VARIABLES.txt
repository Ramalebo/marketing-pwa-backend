RENDER – ENVIRONMENT VARIABLES
Copy each Key and Value into Render → Your backend → Environment

Go to: https://dashboard.render.com → your backend service → Environment
Click "Add Environment Variable" for each row. Replace the YOUR_* placeholders with your real values.
